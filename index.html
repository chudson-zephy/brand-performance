Abandon Rate — Breakdown Notes

≤ 30 seconds ("Short Abandon"): Caller disconnected before reaching 30 seconds in queue. Typically impatient callers or accidental calls. High short-abandon counts can indicate a caller experience issue early in the queue — hold music, IVR friction, or perceived wait length.


> 30 seconds ("True Abandon"): Caller waited beyond 30 seconds before hanging up. These represent genuine frustration with wait time and are the more operationally meaningful metric. Champion & Nash leads in total abandons but the majority are short-abandons (≤30s), suggesting an early-queue experience problem rather than a staffing/capacity issue. StevenKDenny has the highest over-30s abandon rate at 1.6%, indicating real wait-time pressure.


Note on the ≤30s spike: 219 of the 328 total abandons across all brands were logged at exactly 30.000 seconds — likely a system threshold or default timeout value in Five9. These are counted in the "≤30s" bucket. Actual under-30s caller drops are a subset of this number.

Key Brand Observations

Champion & Nash — Watch List

Highest abandon rate at 13.1% and 96 total abandons on 734 calls. Short-abandon % of 12.1% dominates — most callers drop in the first 30s. Service level is the lowest in the portfolio at 73%. Priority brand to investigate queue experience and early-call handling.

Steven K. Denny — Watch List

Second-highest abandon rate at 12.8% with the longest avg call time (4:11) in the portfolio. Also leads in transfers at 138, suggesting potential routing or first-call resolution issues. True abandons (>30s) are highest at 1.6%.

Bonsby — Best Performer

Lowest abandon rate at 5.6% across the highest call volume (1,044 calls). Also holds the shortest avg call time at 2:50. Solid performance heading into the Degree/Bonsby merger — sets a strong operational baseline.

Degree H&C — Solid

Clean numbers at 6.0% abandon and avg call time of 2:56. Lowest transfer volume at 41. Good performance given the merger transition context — suggests the team is stable operationally.

Blue Valley — Strong SVC Level

Best service level in portfolio at 91% and the lowest avg queue wait at 6.8 seconds. Abandon rate of 7.1% is slightly elevated but nearly all are short-abandons (0.3% true abandon). Overall a clean operation.

PSI — High Transfer Volume

Transfer count of 111 is notably high relative to 602 calls (~18% transfer rate). Abandon rate is moderate at 7.0%. Avg call time of 3:25 is above midrange. Worth reviewing transfer reasons to identify routing gaps or agent escalation habits.
